## 5. Description of the Reaction

The suspected reaction was documented as a possible adverse drug reaction associated with amoxicillin. The available case information does not establish definitive causality.

- Antihistamines were administered for management of the reaction.
- The patient reportedly recovered completely by 6 March 2026.
- Rechallenge with amoxicillin was not performed.

The improvement after discontinuation is consistent with a positive dechallenge in the reported narrative; however, this alone does not prove that amoxicillin caused the reaction.

## 9. Preliminary Causality Assessment

The temporal relationship between starting amoxicillin and the onset of itching and rash suggests a possible association.

Factors supporting a possible relationship include:
- The reaction occurred after initiation of the suspected drug.
- The drug was discontinued after the reaction developed.
- Recovery was reported after discontinuation and antihistamine treatment.

Limitations include:
- No laboratory or diagnostic investigation details were provided.
- The exact antihistamine treatment was not specified.
- Alternative causes of the rash were not documented.
- No rechallenge was performed.
- The dose discrepancy in the tutorial remains unresolved.

**Preliminary conclusion:** Amoxicillin is a suspected cause of the reported reaction. A formal causality category or numerical score cannot be assigned reliably from the available information alone. The tutorial describes the reaction as Type B, but this educational classification is not an independently verified causality assessment.
